# 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science undergraduate** with a strong pull toward problems at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, full-stack development, data, and space technology**.

I'm especially interested in places where **data has to be turned into a decision** — whether that's a machine learning model making sense of noisy signals, a data pipeline revealing meaningful patterns, or a software system delivering results under real-world constraints.

🚀 **Space technology** particularly interests me because of the scale and precision of its problems: forecasting, anomaly detection, remote sensing, and intelligent systems where reliability matters.

🧠 **Competitive programming** keeps me grounded in the fundamentals — algorithms, data structures, logical reasoning, and writing efficient solutions.

🌐 **Full-stack development** is where I turn ideas into usable products, allowing me to bridge the gap between an idea and something people can actually interact with.

📊 I'm also exploring **Data Analysis and AI/ML**, learning how to work with data, identify patterns, build models, and communicate insights effectively.

I learn best by **building** — through projects, hackathons, experimentation, technical communities, and problems that force me to understand something rather than simply memorize it.

# 💡 My Approach to Learning

### Build → Break → Understand → Improve

I prefer learning through implementation rather than only following tutorials.

When I encounter something unfamiliar, I try to:

**01 — Understand the problem**

Break down what actually needs to be solved.

**02 — Build something**

Turn the concept into a small working implementation.

**03 — Debug & question**

Understand why something works — and more importantly, why it doesn't.

**04 — Improve**

Look for better approaches, cleaner code, and stronger fundamentals.

This approach has led me toward projects, hackathons, competitive programming, and continuous experimentation across different areas of computer science.
